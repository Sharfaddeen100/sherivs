A. IDENTITY
1. Subsystem Identity
Phase 27 is the CoreGrid Marketplace Platform.
It provides the controlled ecosystem through which authorized parties can discover, evaluate, acquire, install, configure, activate, use, monitor, update, suspend, and remove CoreGrid-compatible products, applications, agents, AI capabilities, integrations, automation packages, educational resources, hardware integrations, services, and institutional extensions.

───

2. Marketplace Mission
Create an ecosystem where CoreGrid becomes:
a platform that can continuously expand without requiring every capability to be built internally by the CoreGrid engineering organization.

───

3. Marketplace Philosophy
The marketplace must prioritize:
• trust
• quality
• interoperability
• security
• transparency
• institutional control
• extensibility
• affordability
• discoverability
• African ecosystem participation

───

4. Marketplace Is Not
Phase 27 is not:
• the application runtime
• the developer platform
• the AI intelligence layer
• the agent operating system
• the automation engine
• the payment ledger
• the identity system
• the educational runtime
• the analytics engine
• the cloud fabric
Those systems remain authoritative in their respective phases.

───

FINAL PHASE 27 CONSTITUTION
The following principles are mandatory:
1. Marketplace products must be explicitly identified.
2. Products must declare permissions.
3. Products must declare data access.
4. Products must declare dependencies.
5. Products must be versioned.
6. Products must be security-scanned.
7. Product packages must be integrity-protected.
8. Marketplace products must respect tenant isolation.
9. Installation must be reversible where technically possible.
10. Marketplace does not own financial truth.
11. Marketplace does not own AI infrastructure.
12. Marketplace does not own agent execution.
13. Marketplace does not own automation execution.
14. Marketplace does not own educational truth.
15. Marketplace does not own identity.
16. Marketplace does not grant authority merely because a product is installed.
17. Administrators retain institutional control.
18. High-risk products require stronger certification.
19. Vendors must remain accountable for their products.
20. Marketplace capabilities must remain replaceable.
21. Product APIs must remain versioned.
22. Marketplace data must remain portable where applicable.
23. African developers and institutions must be first-class ecosystem participants.
24. Localization must not be treated as an afterthought.
25. CoreGrid must remain functional without requiring every marketplace product.
26. The marketplace must expand CoreGrid without corrupting CoreGrid's architectural boundaries.
