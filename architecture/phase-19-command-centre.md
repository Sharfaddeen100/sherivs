A. IDENTITY
A.1 Subsystem Identity
Phase 19 is the CoreGrid Institutional Command Centre.
It is the institutional operational coordination layer through which authorized users can:
•	observe institutional state

•	understand current conditions
•	inspect important events
•	review alerts
•	inspect decisions
•	coordinate responses
•	monitor operational health
•	supervise governed automation
•	review institutional priorities
•	navigate between institutional domains
•	coordinate human and machine workflows
•	access decision intelligence
•	manage institutional situations
The Command Centre is therefore the primary institutional situational-awareness and coordination surface.

A.3 Core Definition
The Command Centre answers:
"What is happening across my institution right now, what requires attention, what decisions are pending, and what coordinated response should happen next?"
It does not independently determine institutional truth.
It does not independently execute unrestricted actions.
