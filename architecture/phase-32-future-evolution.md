PHASE 32 — COREGRID FUTURE EVOLUTION ENGINE
A. IDENTITY
32.1 Subsystem Identity
Phase Name: CoreGrid Future Evolution Engine
Phase Number: 32
Architectural Role: Evolutionary governance and future-capability layer of CoreGrid.
Primary Responsibility:
Enable CoreGrid to evolve safely, continuously, experimentally, and backward-compatibly as technology, education, institutions, infrastructure, regulations, devices, AI capabilities, and global operating environments change.
The Future Evolution Engine does not replace existing CoreGrid phases.
It does not become a second automation engine.
It does not become a second AI intelligence layer.
It does not become a second security system.
It provides the controlled evolutionary mechanisms through which future capabilities can be researched, introduced, evaluated, graduated, deprecated, migrated, or rejected.

E. CORE EVOLUTION PRINCIPLES
32.5 Evolution Principles
Every future capability must follow:
Principle 1 — No Silent Mutation
Production behavior must never change invisibly.
Principle 2 — Experiment Before Graduation
Experimental capabilities must be isolated before production adoption.
Principle 3 — Evidence Before Expansion
A capability must demonstrate measurable value before broad deployment.
Principle 4 — Compatibility Before Convenience
New versions must preserve compatibility wherever reasonably possible.
Principle 5 — Reversible Before Irreversible
Prefer changes that can be rolled back.
Principle 6 — Explicit Before Implicit
Evolutionary changes require explicit versioning and governance.
Principle 7 — Tenant Safety
Experimental capabilities must never unexpectedly affect institutions.
Principle 8 — Human Governance Where Appropriate
High-impact evolutionary changes require human approval.
Principle 9 — Security Is Continuous
Future capabilities inherit CoreGrid security requirements from the beginning.
Principle 10 — Architecture Must Remain Composable
New capabilities must integrate through contracts rather than architectural coupling.

EB. FINAL PRINCIPLE
CoreGrid must never attempt to become “future-proof” by guessing exactly what the future will look like.
It becomes future-ready by making itself changeable without becoming fragile.
The architecture should therefore follow one final rule:
Build today's CoreGrid with enough structure that tomorrow's CoreGrid can be fundamentally different without having to destroy today's CoreGrid.
Phase 32 is therefore the evolutionary safety layer of the entire CoreGrid architecture.
It closes the current architecture without closing the future.
