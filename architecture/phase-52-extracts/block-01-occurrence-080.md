BLOCK 01 — THE THREE EXPERIENCE WORLDS

Every organization in CoreGrid gets three primary experience surfaces.

1. Public Experience

The organization's public website/presence.

Examples:

homepage

services

products

locations

about

contact

booking

public content

public AI assistant

public map

public offers



---

2. Operational Experience

The organization's internal workspace.

Used by:

owner

administrator

manager

employee

finance

operations

AI agents

specialized staff


This can be highly adaptive.


---

3. Customer Experience

The organization's customer-facing application/service environment.

Examples:

ordering food

hotel booking

spa booking

school parent portal

appointment management

transportation

payments

loyalty

communication


This should be much more stable than the internal workspace.
