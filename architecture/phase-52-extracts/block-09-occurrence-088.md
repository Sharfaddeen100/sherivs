BLOCK 09 — THE UNIVERSAL COREGRID USER

Now your vision becomes much bigger than organization software.

A CoreGrid user should have a Universal Identity.

One account can have:

User
│
├── Personal Reality
│
├── Organization Membership A
│
├── Organization Membership B
│
├── Organization Membership C
│
├── Customer Relationships
│
├── Services
│
├── Purchases
│
├── Bookings
│
├── Devices
│
├── AI Agents
│
├── Preferences
│
└── Permissions

The user does not need separate CoreGrid accounts.
