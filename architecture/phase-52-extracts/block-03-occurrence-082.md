When an organization joins CoreGrid:

ORGANIZATION ENTERS
       ↓
DOMAIN IDENTIFICATION
       ↓
DOMAIN INVARIANTS LOADED
       ↓
MANDATORY EXPERIENCE GENERATED
       ↓
ORGANIZATION INTERVIEW
       ↓
QUESTIONNAIRE
       ↓
DOCUMENT/SYSTEM ANALYSIS
       ↓
REALITY MODEL
       ↓
CUSTOM EXPERIENCE GENERATED
       ↓
ADMIN REVIEW
       ↓
ACTIVATION

So the experience is:

DOMAIN BASELINE
+
ORGANIZATION REALITY
+
ORGANIZATION PREFERENCES
+
ROLES
+
POLICIES
+
OBJECTIVES
+
LOCATION
+
WORKFLOWS
+
AGENTS

ORGANIZATION EXPERIENCE
