Purpose:
Block 4 defines how CoreGrid expands from one instantiated runtime (Scholar) into an intelligence platform capable of understanding, modeling, and generating operational environments for any organization, industry, civilization, or domain.

"Which dashboard should I build?"

"What exists here? What entities interact? What rules govern them? What changes? What problems occur? What intelligence is required?"
