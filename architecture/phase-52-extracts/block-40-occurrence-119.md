UNIVERSAL CIVILIZATION FOUNDATION + COREGRID MONOREPO

I would make one important correction before we lock Block 01:

> All people and entities can have a CoreGrid identity and representation, but CoreGrid should not legally or semantically "own" everything.



CoreGrid should be the universal identity and intelligence substrate.

An organization owns its business entities and relationships according to its rights and agreements. A person owns/control their personal data and permissions. CoreGrid governs the infrastructure, identity, policies, discovery, intelligence, transactions, and relationships between them.

That distinction is foundational.
