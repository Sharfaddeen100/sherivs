The key architectural law of Block 02 will be:
Identity establishes existence. Authority establishes permission. Policy establishes conditions. Tenant isolation establishes boundaries. CoreGrid executes only after all four agree.
So the construction sequence is now fixed:
Block 01 → complete.
Block 02 → next construction unit.
 Then Block 03, Block 04, all the way through Block 18.
Only after Block 18 do we bind the 55 phases.
That prevents the exact architectural failure we are trying to avoid: building a huge collection of features first and discovering later that they do not share a universal substrate.

















Yes. We should correct the construction standard here.
Block 01 was the conceptual specification, not the complete machine-level specification. We should not repeat that mistake for Block 02.
For Block 02, we will build the entire security/identity substrate down to contracts, enforcement points, data boundaries, lifecycle, failure behavior, APIs, storage, testing and AI-agent integration.
Also, you listed RBAC twice; I will treat that as one RBAC layer and include the other major authorization/security models around it.
