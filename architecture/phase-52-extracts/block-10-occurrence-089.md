BLOCK 10 — MULTI-ORGANIZATION REALITY

Suppose one person is:

Owner → Restaurant A
Employee → Hotel B
Customer → Spa C
Parent → School D
Developer → CoreGrid Marketplace
Individual → Personal CoreGrid

CoreGrid should understand all of these memberships simultaneously.

But it must keep their authority boundaries separate.
