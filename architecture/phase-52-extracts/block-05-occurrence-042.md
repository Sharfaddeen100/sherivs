BLOCK 5 — COREGRID REALITY SIMULATION UNIVERSE, CAUSAL INTELLIGENCE, FUTURE PREDICTION ENGINE, SCENARIO GENERATION AND ORGANIZATION EVOLUTION SYSTEM
Subsystem: CoreGrid Future Intelligence Layer
Purpose:
Block 5 defines the intelligence layer that allows CoreGrid to move beyond understanding the present reality into understanding possible futures.
A normal software system records what happened.
An AI assistant explains what is happening.
A predictive system estimates what may happen.
But CoreGrid is designed to become a reality simulation intelligence system.
Its purpose is:
To create a living computational representation of an organization, understand the forces affecting it, simulate possible futures, identify risks before they occur, and help organizations make better decisions before reality forces them to react.
