BLOCK 04 — THE ORGANIZATION INTERVIEW ENGINE

This should itself be an AI-guided experience.

When a new organization joins:

> "Let's understand your organization."



CoreGrid asks questions adaptively.

Not a 500-question boring form.

The interview engine begins with perhaps:

Organization identity

What do you do?

What services/products do you provide?

Where do you operate?

How many locations?

Who are your customers?


Then CoreGrid dynamically generates the next questions.

For a restaurant:

> Do you accept reservations?



If yes:

> How are tables organized?



Then:

> Do you support delivery?



If yes:

> Which delivery channels?



Then:

> Do you manage your own drivers?



And so on.

The interview becomes an ontology discovery conversation.
