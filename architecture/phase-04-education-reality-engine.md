Universal African Educational Runtime
File:architecture/phase-04-education-reality-engine.md
Architecture: CoreGrid v2
Status: Scope-Locked Production Architecture
Subsystem: CoreGrid Scholar Educational Runtime
Primary responsibility: Model, execute and continuously understand the complete educational and operational reality of an African educational institution.

───

1. SYSTEM IDENTITY
CoreGrid Scholar Educational Runtime is the foundational runtime that allows CoreGrid to operate as an actual educational system rather than merely a collection of school-management features.
It answers:
What does this institution look like?
How does this institution operate?
What does this institution teach?
Who participates in education?
Where does education happen?
When does education happen?
Which resources are involved?
Which rules govern it?
How do people, places, classes, subjects, schedules, devices, facilities and learning activities relate to one another?

The runtime must be capable of representing an institution without requiring CoreGrid engineers to interview the institution and hard-code its operating model.

───

2. CORE PRINCIPLE
CoreGrid Scholar is:
configuration-driven, policy-driven, context-driven, relationship-driven and AI-native.
The institution defines its operating reality.
CoreGrid interprets that reality.
CoreGrid does not dictate a single school structure.

───

3. AFRICA-FIRST PRINCIPLE
The runtime must be designed primarily around the diversity of African educational systems.
It must not secretly assume:
•	British school structures
•	American school structures
•	Cambridge structures
•	International Baccalaureate structures
•	European semester models
•	Western grading systems
•	Western classroom assumptions
Those may be supported later as optional configurations.
They must never become the architectural foundation.
